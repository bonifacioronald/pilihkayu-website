# Shot list for v1 (Home, About, products)

Exact shots, counts, formats and folders. Shoot on your phone; send originals (not via WhatsApp).
Upload on GitHub to the folder named for each item (Add file → Upload files). File names don't matter.

## Rules for every shot

- **Light:** daylight or bright even factory light. No flash, no filters, no portrait mode.
- **Steady:** phone on a tripod, a stack of boxes, or braced against something. 1× lens.
- **Clean:** sweep dust and clutter out of frame; no bottles, phones, rubbish bags.
- **People:** only with their consent; faces optional, hands are better.
- **Photos:** JPG or HEIC, as shot.
- **Clips:** portrait 9:16, 4K or 1080p, MP4 or MOV, under 25 MB each. No text, music or talking.
  Slow, steady movement. Clips can be **3–6 s**: I loop them seamlessly (I play them forward
  then backward, so the end never jumps), as long as the camera does not move suddenly.
